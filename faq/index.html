Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the most frequently asked questions about FreeDiamond.in. Learn how to earn and redeem diamonds easily.

Q: Free Fire mein diamond kaise milega free mein?

A: FreeDiamond.in app par MISSIONS tab me jakar 5 daily mission links visit karo (15 seconds wait karo). Diamonds automatically aapke wallet me add ho jate hain jise aap direct apne Free Fire UID par redeem kar sakte ho.

Q: Free Fire UID se diamond kaise milta hai?

A: FreeDiamond.in par apna numeric Free Fire UID enter karo, REDEEM tab mein diamond package select karo aur submit karo. System directly us Free Fire UID par diamond top up process karta hai.

Q: Free fire diamond uid top up free mein kaise kare?

A: FreeDiamond.in par 5 daily missions complete karke diamonds earn karo aur REDEEM tab se 520, 1060, 2180 ya 5600 diamond package choose karke claim karo - koi payment ya password nahi chahiye.

Q: Free diamond uid kya hota hai?

A: Free diamond uid matlab aapka Free Fire player profile ID number (e.g. 123456789) jis par aap free diamonds receive karte ho. Koi login ya account password ki jarurat nahi hoti.

Q: Kya FreeDiamond.in Free Fire Max ke liye bhi kaam karta hai?

A: Haan. Free Fire aur Free Fire Max dono ek hi UID share karte hain. FreeDiamond.in par redeem karne par diamonds dono games mein reflect ho jaate hain.
