Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Welcome to FreeDiamond.in. We respect your privacy and provide a 100% Zero-Login, transparent reward experience.

1. Zero Personal Data Collected

We never ask for or collect sensitive personal data such as your real name, phone number, email address, or gaming account passwords. The only inputs processed are: (a) Your numerical Free Fire Player UID when submitting a redemption request, and (b) Client-side local storage variables to track your completed daily missions and diamond balance.

2. Local Storage & Session State

Your diamond wallet balance and daily task completion states are stored securely inside your own browser's LocalStorage. This ensures fast, login-free operation without storing private profiles on central servers.

3. Payout Data Security

When you redeem diamonds, only your Free Fire Player UID and the requested diamond package are transmitted to our secure Firestore database for payout processing. We never share, sell, or rent player UIDs to any third party.
